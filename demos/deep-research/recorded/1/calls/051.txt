{"summary":"Lake Taneycomo is a good, beginner-friendly option for kids because MDC stocks about 560,000 rainbow and 15,000 brown trout a year (rainbows are ~90% of the catch), and the lake is fishable year-round. Where you fish matters. The first ~3 miles below Table Rock Dam (to Fall Creek) are artificial lures and flies only, with a 12-20 inch rainbow release slot, and the first 1.5 miles swing between deep fast water during generation and shallow water otherwise, so that stretch is poor for young kids. Below Fall Creek, natural and scented baits (worms, PowerBait) are allowed with no length limit on rainbows, which suits beginners. A trout permit is required to possess trout (daily limit 4); the upper lake and anything upstream of the Hwy 65 bridge also need one. Kid-friendly access: MDC lists covered, accessible public fishing docks at Cooper Creek Access, Rockaway Beach Access (Hwy 176, open 4 AM-10 PM, 417-895-6880) and Empire Electric Park, plus public docks at Branson Lakeside RV Park and along the Branson Landing lakefront. Lilley's Landing Resort & Marina (family-owned since 1982) rents jon boats and pontoons, sells temporary licenses, offers guides, and per a forum poster allows dock fishing for non-guests; its guide Phil Lilley says novices with worms and PowerBait will catch fish. Fall Creek has a pay-to-fish dock per a forum poster, and Fall Creek Marina rents boats with child-size life vests. Below Rockaway Beach the lake shifts to bass, bluegill, redear sunfish and crappie, which MDC calls good for kids and beginners. Short 2-hour beginner/kid guided trips exist but are guide-marketed, with prices from about $325 for 4 hours. Caveats: a forum thread notes kids want steady action and trout fishing can be only fair on some days; MapQuest showed Lilley's as Closed, probably outdated, so confirm.
